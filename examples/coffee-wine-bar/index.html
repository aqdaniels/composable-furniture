Build sequence and schedule

  1. Week 0: order the Semihandmade olive parts (6–8 weeks). Book the electrician. Ask TCL whether the B422D vents from the front.
  2. Week 0–1: electrician adds a dedicated outlet behind the cooler bay.
  3. About week 7: order the IKEA, Target, TCL and hardware parts so they land before the olive parts.
  4. Hang the wallpaper first, from 40" up to the ceiling, so it runs behind the shelves.
  5. Hang the base cabinet on its suspension rail with the legs set high; check the 24¼" bay and 34½" clearance.
  6. Fit the olive end panel at the left wall, then the scribed filler at the right.
  7. Cut the 98" countertop into the 55" top and two 4" strips (43" + 12"). Fix the top, then the strips against the wall.
  8. Finish the walnut shelves, then mount them into studs at 54" and 72", and the two LED bars under the lower one.
  9. Fit pulls and toekick. Slide in the cooler, level it, let it stand 4 hours, plug it in.
Tools

Drill/driver, stud finder, level, track or circular saw for the countertop, wallpaper smoother and knife.

Time

About 1½ days once the parts are in, plus the electrician.

Skill

Intermediate. The countertop cut and scribing the filler are the fussy parts.

Risks and swaps

Risks
  • The cooler is the biggest unknown. Its venting isn't stated anywhere I could read. If TCL says it vents from the back, it can't be enclosed and the bay needs a different unit.
  • Only 0.3" of height to spare over the cooler. Measure the floor for level before building.
  • IKEA makes no 21" or 27" drawer fronts, so 24" drawers are the widest that fit with the cooler; the leftover becomes a 6" olive filler.
  • The wallpaper's panel size and washability aren't on its page.
  • Semihandmade's small-order rules conflict; confirm the fee.
Swaps
  • Skip the wait (cheaper): drop the Semihandmade order (−$455 including the fee) and paint IKEA cover panels and trim olive yourself.
  • Cheaper shelves: IKEA BERGSHULT walnut-effect shelves on brackets are far cheaper but rated 44 lb per 47¼" shelf, too little for two rows of bottles.
  • Stone counter (upgrade): IKEA MÖCKLARP porcelain, ordered and installed through the store.
  • Wider still (30" drawers): fits only if the cooler bay shrinks to 24", leaving almost no room for uneven walls. Not recommended.
  • Wine rack: the cooler holds 20 bottles. The shelves now hold the bottles on show.
